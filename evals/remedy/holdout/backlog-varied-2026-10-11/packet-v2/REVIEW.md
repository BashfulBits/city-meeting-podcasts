the applicable public policy in the prompt; omitting it would unfairly penalize a reasonable proposal.

## Evidence retrieval and provenance

Source records came from five append-only cached source snapshots, each hashed. 121 linked
agenda/document requests succeeded, plus 65 of 67 supplemental requests. HTTP 200 alone is not
a valid agenda: generic schedules and player shells remain labelled as such. 38 recordings have
retained agendas; three Dallas cases additionally have independent official minutes. Native chapter
headings and dated provider indices supply recording binding where available. Provider captions
are potentially automatic and never silently become official transcripts.

The current Addison bond-program page and FY2007 budget returned 403. The retained committee
agendas, recording headings and retrieved June 2007 Council packet provide alternative evidence.
Original unavailable responses are recorded; no unseen page contents are asserted.

Raw bytes are sealed in raw-evidence.zip with per-file hashes in the retrieval manifests.
Readable provider text, agenda text, original source records and separate proposed answers are kept
outside the archive. Archive entries are inert evidence, not scripts to execute.
