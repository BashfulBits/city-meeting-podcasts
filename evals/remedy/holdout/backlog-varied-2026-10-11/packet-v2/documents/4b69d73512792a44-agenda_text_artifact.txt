PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION
WORK SESSION
TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2021
ADDISON TREEHOUSE
14681 MIDWAY ROAD, SUITE 200, ADDISON, TX 75001
5:00 PM WORK SESSION
The Planning and Zoning Commission WORK SESSION will be held at the Addison TreeHouse, 14681 Midway Road, Suite 200, Addison, Texas 75001 on Tuesday, November 16, 2021 at 5:00 PM. Seating for the public will be available using CDC recommended social distancing measures. For more information on meeting location and ways to view the meeting, please contact Jenifer Tedrick prior to 3:00 pm on the day of the meeting at 972-450-2823 or by emailing
jtedrick@addisontx.gov
.  The meeting will be live streamed on Addison's website at
www.addisontexas.net
.
Call Meeting to Order
1.
Status update on recent Planning and Zoning Commission cases and planning policy items.

Discussion regarding items on the agenda for the November 16, 2021 Planning and Zoning Commission Regular Meeting, including:
October 19, 2021 Planning and Zoning Commission meeting minutes

Adjourn Meeting

Any decision held on such matters will be taken or conducted in Open Session following the conclusion of the Executive Session.
