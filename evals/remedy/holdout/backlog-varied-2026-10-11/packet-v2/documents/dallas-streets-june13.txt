Discussion Items:
The Department of Public Works (PBW) introduced their staff and started the presentation
by defining the Needs Inventory, explaining criteria for projects to be placed on the Needs
Inventory list for each asset managed by Public Works, and listing the project categories
that are on the Needs Inventory. Also, PBW presented the overall City Needs per asset
and their percentages along with a detailed data presentation of the Needs share per
Council District for each asset. Public Works staff continued with defining Citywide
projects and categories that would be considered as “Citywide” by referencing the April
2023 Memo to Mayor and Members of the City Council, “2024 Bond Program Development
– Definition of “Citywide” Projects” . Then, they presented a Data comparison for Alleys on
the Needs Inventory per Council District as well as improved versus unimproved alleys.
Afterwards, they covered the Sidewalk Master Plan focus areas and the top priority
bridges list on the Needs Inventory. Sidewalks and Bridges priority lists were also
provided. In the end, staff presented several budget scenarios with the proposed
allocations breakdown per each asset. Finally, staff opened the questions and answers
session and answered Subcommittee Members’ questions below.
