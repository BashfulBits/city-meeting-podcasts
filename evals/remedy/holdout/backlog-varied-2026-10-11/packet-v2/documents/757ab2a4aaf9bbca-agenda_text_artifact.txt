7/23/26, 11:27 AM Print All TIRZ #1 Board Meeting Item # 2.a. Meeting Date: 07/28/2026 Department: Economic Development Key Focus Areas: Economic Development and Revitalization AGENDA CAPTION: Present, discuss, and consider action on the recommendation of the Project and Financing Plan for Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone #1. BACKGROUND: The Board of Directors of the Town of Addison's Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone (TIRZ) #1 is charged with preparing and recommending a Project and Financing Plan for the Zone. This item presents the Final Project and Financing Plan for the Board’s review and recommendation to the City Council, which will consider the Plan for approval during the Regular Meeting. On July 14, 2026, the City Council adopted Ordinance No. 026-028, known as the Creation Ordinance, establishing Reinvestment Zone Number One, Town of Addison (TIRZ #1). This builds on prior Council direction provided over the last two years. At a Tax Increment Financing (TIF) 101 briefing on July 23, 2024, Council directed staff to evaluate a TIRZ for the Transit-Oriented Development. At its November 4, 2025 City Council Work Session Meeting, Council directed preparation of the Project and Financing Plan and gave direction on the Town's stair-step participation rate. Additionally, a TIRZ was recommended specifically for this area by the 2019 Addison Circle Special Area Study. The 2017 Inwood Corridor Enhancement Zone Study identified TIRZs more generally as a tool to support redevelopment and infrastructure reinvestment. Separately, the Town is negotiating an interlocal agreement with Dallas Area Rapid Transit (DART) under which DART would contribute to the TIRZ #1 Fund on terms comparable to a participating unit, anticipated at approximately 60% for a term still under negotiation. This contribution would be made through the interlocal agreement rather than as a Chapter 311 tax increment participation, as DART is not a taxing unit. Upon the Board’s recommendation, the Final Project and Financing Plan will be presented to the City Council for approval. FISCAL IMPACT The Final Project and Financing Plan reflects the Zone as created. The tax increment base is the appraised value of taxable real property within the Zone as of January 1, 2026, estimated at approximately $1.48 billion. The Plan identifies approximately $183.6 million in eligible project costs, allocated across categories eligible under Chapter 311: economic development grants (30%), pedestrian enhancements (18%), parking and transit (15%), street and intersection (15%), open space and public facilities (15%), public utilities (5%), and administrative costs (2%). These figures are estimates and may be revised, with savings in one category applied to another, and individual allocations brought to the TIRZ Board and City Council for consideration. RECOMMENDATION Staff recommends approval.
