Child Care Needs
• During the declaration of emergency related to COVID-19, many child
  care centers were closed or required to serve only children of
  essential workers
• Child care providers lost significant revenue and many are still closed
  or at reduced capacity
• As businesses open up in Arlington, there is an increased need for
  affordable and available child care slots to help families restore and
  maintain income
• At the last committee meeting, members discussed using available
  CDBG-CV funds to support child care services
Child Care Survey

 • Child Care Associates (CCA) has been collecting data from child care providers in Tarrant
   County. The following is survey data from June 8.
 • Of 415 sites reporting, 103 were from Arlington (25%)
 • Of 9,879 child care slots, 2,276 were from Arlington (23%)
 • In Tarrant County, 67% of child care providers were open and 33% closed due to COVID-19
 • In Arlington, 74% of providers were open and 26% were closed, representing about 592
   child care slots closed due to COVID-19
 • More centers are opening each week
