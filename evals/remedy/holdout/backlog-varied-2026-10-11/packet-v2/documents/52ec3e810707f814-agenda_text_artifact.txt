PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION
WORK SESSION
TUESDAY, JANUARY 18, 2022
ADDISON TREEHOUSE
14681 MIDWAY ROAD, SUITE 200, ADDISON, TX 75001
5:30 PM WORK SESSION
The Planning and Zoning Commission WORK SESSION will be held at the Addison TreeHouse, 14681 Midway Road, Suite 200, Addison, Texas 75001 on Tuesday, January 18, 2022 at 5:30 PM. Seating for the public will be available using CDC recommended social distancing measures. For more information on the meeting location and ways to view the meeting, please contact Jenifer Tedrick prior to 3:00 pm on the day of the meeting at 972-450-2823 or by emailing
jtedrick@addisontx.gov
.  The meeting will be live streamed on Addison's website at
www.addisontexas.net
.
Call Meeting to Order
1.
Status update on recent Planning ad Zoning Commission cases and planning policy items.
2.
Discussion regarding items on the agenda for the January 18, 2022 Planning and Zoning Commission Regular Meeting, including:
Appointment of Chair and Vice Chair for the Planning and Zoning Commission
December 21, 2021 Planning and Zoning Commission Meeting Minutes
Final Plat and Replat, Block D of the Vitruvian Park Addition (R2021-02)
Replat, Block 1, Lot 1 of the Prairie Medical Addition and Site Plan for a medical office building (R2021-03 & SP2021-01)
Preliminary Plat, Block C, Lot 1 of the Vitruvian Park Addition (PP2021-01)
2021 Planning and Zoning Commission Annual Report
2022 Planning and Development Work Plan
Adjourn Meeting
NOTE: The Planning & Zoning Commission reserves the right to meet in Executive Session closed to the public at any time in the course of this meeting to discuss matters listed on the agenda, as authorized by the Texas Open Meetings Act, Texas Government Code, Chapter 551, including §551.071 (private consultation with the attorney for the City). Any decision held on such matters will be taken or conducted in Open Session following the conclusion of the Executive Session.
