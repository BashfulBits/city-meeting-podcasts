# Additional Fort Worth program context

Read-only official-domain search on 2026-10-11 found these contextual sources after the main
packet was sealed. Search-index excerpts are retained here as research leads, not downloaded
full documents or exact recording bindings. Direct retrieval of the Library page and FY2008
budget returned 403. No unseen pages, specific episode contents or institutional owner are inferred.

- Official FY2007 cable budget describes recording municipal meetings, producing special-interest
  programs and a televised City Page, and carrying private/community-access programming.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/the-fwlab/documents/budget-analysis/fy2007-budget/fy2007-p-special-funds-adopted.pdf
- Official FY2008 budget lists Minority Leaders and Citizens Council among cable programming,
  alongside cultural and community events. This is contextual evidence, not a Council convening.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/planning-data-analytics/documents/budget-amp-analysis/fy2008-budget/fy2008-p-special-funds-adopted-2007-08.pdf
- The official Library Memory Box service page has a Fort Worth Forward feature. This supports
  the possibility of municipal information in the series, without binding Episode15 or Episode19.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/library/services/10-memory-box
