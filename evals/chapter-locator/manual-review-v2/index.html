How to score the boundaries

Each page is one published chapter boundary. Its time field starts at the median of available model predictions, with each model counted once regardless of how many prompt variants were run. Save that time if it looks right, or click the first word of the new discussion to adjust it.

Model and route names are hidden while you label. Afterward, the saved time is compared with each available response separately. A blank suggestion means no response was confidently matched to this chapter’s agenda item.

Use the left and right arrow keys to move between boundaries. Press C for core, O for optional, or U for unsure. In the always-open agenda section, check every agenda item at this transition and any other published chapters that share it. Their saved time and agenda-item selections are combined across the linked boundaries.

Optionally classify how much this boundary matters to following the meeting: core, optional to omit, or unsure. This is separate from whether a model found the boundary at the right time.

If a provider chapter has no corresponding spoken segment, mark No spoken segment. If the transcript is missing near the provider time, mark Transcript gap. Both are excluded from timing and recall scoring; a transcript gap does not mean the discussion did not happen.
